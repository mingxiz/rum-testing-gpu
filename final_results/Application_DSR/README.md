# Application_DSR: the choice-overload data of Dean, Ravindran and Stoye (2022), n = 25

`results_dsr.csv`: the Hong&ndash;Li rationalizability test on the DSR data (520 respondents, 301 menus), one row per run,
generated by `python validation/make_final_results_apps.py` from the records in `replication/empirical_dsr_n25/results/`.
Both observation sets are included: **alt0**, the reference option's frequency in each menu (M = 301), and **full**,
every alternative of every menu (M = 901).

## Criterion: abs 5e-4, refined within the run

One tolerance, `abs_tol` (rel 0), governs both halves of the test, for alt0 and full alike:

1. **The dist(p&#770;) certificate.** Column generation stops when UB &minus; LB &le; abs_tol. The threshold band
   &tau; = scale &times; [LB, UB], with scale = 1 + n<sup>&minus;1/3</sup>&radic;n = 3.8357 for n = 520, therefore narrows as the
   tolerance tightens: it is 3.84 times the certificate gap wide.
2. **Every bootstrap draw.** A draw stops early as *below* once its UB &lt; &tau;<sub>lo</sub>, or *above* once its certified
   LB &ge; &tau;<sub>hi</sub>. A draw that is neither runs on to UB &minus; LB &le; abs_tol and is counted *undecided* (indicator 1,
   the conservative side). The p-value is the bracket [n_above, n_above + n_undecided] / B.
3. **Refinement.** The GPU runs (`rum.bootstrap`, `replication/empirical_dsr_n25/run_bootstrap.py`) then take up to two
   tenfold tighter rounds within the same run, 5e-5 and 5e-6: p&#770; is certified again if its bracket is wider than
   the new tolerance (that narrows the band), and every draw still undecided is solved again.

Before any draw is solved, each draw's distance to the RUM point fitted at p&#770; bounds its distance from above; a draw
whose bound is already below &tau;<sub>lo</sub> is decided with no solve. The rest restart from the p&#770; solution.
CGBFQP-GPU (the solver's automatic choice), multinomial_reject draws, seed 0:

| obs | B | certificate gap | &tau; band width | screened | below / above / undecided | p-value | time | 95 % interval | interval time |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| alt0 | 10,000 | 2.9e-07 | 1.1e-06 | 2,378 | 8,917 / 1,083 / 0 | 0.1083 | 688 s (11.5 min) | [-0.0476, &infin;) | 237 s (3.9 min), 592 draws |
| alt0 | 1,000 | 2.9e-07 | 1.1e-06 | 244 | 892 / 108 / 0 | 0.1080 | 69.8 s | [-0.0441, &infin;) | 24.9 s, 62 draws |
| full | 10,000 | 2.3e-07 | 8.9e-07 | 1,055 | 8,608 / 1,392 / 0 | 0.1392 | 1499 s (25.0 min) | [-0.0902, &infin;) | 680 s (11.3 min), 649 draws |
| full | 1,000 | 4.7e-04 | 1.8e-03 | 116 | 857 / 143 / 0 | 0.1430 | 152 s (2.5 min) | [-0.0963, &infin;) | 67.8 s, 65 draws |

- **Every draw is decided**, in one run. Without the refinement, deciding every draw takes three complete runs, at
  abs 5e-4, 1e-4 and 1e-5 (2 draws are undecided at 5e-4 on alt0, 13 on full).
- **The interval** is the one-sided [dist(p&#770;) &minus; q / r<sub>n</sub>, &infin;) with q the 95 % quantile of
  (dist(P<sub>b</sub>) &minus; dist(p&#770;)) / &epsilon;<sub>n</sub>; the reported lower end is the conservative end of a
  certified bracket 9.0e-05 (alt0) and 1.6e-04 (full) wide at B = 10,000. It contains 0 on both sets, consistent with
  p &gt; 0.05. At B = 10,000 the 1,000 draws with the largest upper bounds were solved again to abs 5e-6; the exact
  lower end lies inside the reported bracket on both sets.

**Result: alt0 p = 0.1083, full p = 0.1392, every draw decided; do not reject RUM at 5 %.**

The AI application runs the same procedure; there every draw is decided below by the screen. See
`../Application_AI/README.md`.

## Also in the CSV

- CGIP and CGIP-heur (random insertion, R = 10, IP at stalls) at B = 10,000 on the CPU cluster, one uninterrupted
  job per run on an Ice Lake node with 24 Gurobi threads, made with an earlier version of the CGIP code
  (`replication/cgip/cluster_records/Application_DSR/`; `replication/cgip/run_cgip.py` re-runs them with this
  release). They draw at abs 5e-4 and then settle dist(p&#770;) and every undecided draw at abs 1e-7, and reach the
  same decisions:

| obs | solver | below / above / undecided | p-value | total |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| alt0 | CGIP | 8,917 / 1,083 / 0 | 0.1083 | 23.54 h |
| alt0 | CGIP-heur | 8,917 / 1,083 / 0 | 0.1083 | 7.90 h |
| full | CGIP | 8,608 / 1,392 / 0 | 0.1392 | 96.77 h |
| full | CGIP-heur | 8,608 / 1,392 / 0 | 0.1392 | 56.88 h |

  The chained 24 h day jobs that reproduce these runs, and the earlier CGIP-heur runs with R = 1, are in that
  folder's `summary.csv`, not here.
- BM-ADMM is not in the DSR comparison: at n = 25 it needs float32, and on alt0 it did not certify dist(p&#770;)
  within a 4 h cap (14,422 s, 132,000 iterations, bracket [1.3967, 4.5883]; CGBFQP-GPU: 0.8 s).
