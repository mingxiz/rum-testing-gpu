# Application_AI: LLM choice data, n = 18

`results_ai.csv` holds the eight GPU runs of the AI application: BM-ADMM and CGBFQP-GPU, each on the four LLM data
sets (gpt-5.6-luna and deepseek-v4-flash with 100 replies per menu, gpt-6 luna without reasoning and GMI Cloud
with 2,000), under the Hong&ndash;Li rationalizability test with **B = 10,000 bootstrap draws from the
multinomial_reject respondent bootstrap** and a per-solve tolerance of **abs 5e-4**, run through `rum.bootstrap`
(`replication/empirical_ai_n18/run_bootstrap.py`), and the CPU cluster runs of CGIP and CGIP-heur on gpt-5.6-luna
and deepseek-v4-flash. It is generated by `python validation/make_final_results_apps.py` from the run records in
`replication/empirical_ai_n18/results/`; the column definitions are in `../README.md`.

## What was done

1. **Data.** Each of the 8,807 menus of 18 Apple Watch configurations (all 153 binary and 816 ternary menus, a 3 %
   sample of every cardinality 4&ndash;17 and the grand menu) was put to gpt-5.6-luna and to deepseek-v4-flash 100
   times each, and to gpt-6 luna without reasoning and the GMI Cloud model 2,000 times each, every time in a
   separate call. The replies are read into a panel by `replication/empirical_ai_n18/replies.py`: one row per reply,
   with the replicate index as the respondent (100 or 2,000 pseudo-respondents), the menu shown and the product
   picked; a reply that does not parse is dropped. The choice frequencies p&#770;(x | D) have one cell per (menu,
   member alternative), M = 73,492 cells. The replies are in `final_data/Application_AI/raw_data/`, as the tables
   read from the models' jsonl replies and written by `replies.save_table`: `gpt5.6-luna.npz`,
   `deepseekv4-flash.npz`, `gpt6luna_noreason.npz` and `gmicloud.npz` (1.5, 1.7, 29 and 31 MB).

   | data set | model | replies | unparseable, dropped | cells M | data fingerprint |
   |---|---|---|---|---|---|
   | gpt-5.6-luna | gpt-5.6-luna | 880,700 | 0 | 73,492 | `0416cd969f09dee8` |
   | deepseek-v4-flash | deepseek/deepseek-v4-flash (OpenRouter) | 880,700 | 165 | 73,492 | `ae678d5f13a1619a` |
   | gpt-6-luna-noreason | gpt-6 luna, no reasoning | 17,614,000 | 0 | 73,492 | `1ff75faae82e3a68` |
   | gmi-cloud | GMI Cloud | 17,614,000 | 2,414 | 73,492 | `db395e88217afd61` |

2. **Bootstrap draws: multinomial_reject, B = 10,000, seed 0.** Each draw resamples the pseudo-respondents with
   replacement, keeps each respondent's answers to every menu together, and recomputes the frequencies; a resample
   that leaves some menu without any answer is redrawn. This is the bootstrap of the DSR runs, drawn by
   `solver/inference/draws.py` (draws fingerprints: gpt-5.6-luna `be60cf2019b92f26`, deepseek-v4-flash `3f68c90d7e594502`, gpt-6 luna `7f270d9c7674cb8c`,
   GMI Cloud `d11284a7f3384f9f`). No resample was ever redrawn:
   every menu was answered by at least 94 of the 100 pseudo-respondents on the 100-reply sets, and by at least
   1,995 of the 2,000 on the others. The draws are rebuilt 128 MB at a time instead of being held as one
   10,000 &times; 73,492 array (5.9 GB), and both solvers received the identical draws.

3. **Distance certificate.** Each solver certifies dist(p&#770;), the distance from p&#770; to the RUM polytope, to a bracket
   [LB, UB] with UB &minus; LB &le; 5e-4 (rel 0). The threshold band is &tau; = scale &times; [LB, UB], with
   r<sub>n</sub> = &radic;n, &epsilon;<sub>n</sub> = n<sup>&minus;1/3</sup> and scale = 1 + &epsilon;<sub>n</sub>r<sub>n</sub>: n = 100 on the 100-reply sets
   (r<sub>n</sub> = 10, &epsilon;<sub>n</sub> = 0.2154, scale = 3.1544) and n = 2,000 on the others (r<sub>n</sub> = 44.72,
   &epsilon;<sub>n</sub> = 0.0794, scale = 4.5495).

4. **Draw decisions.** Each draw p<sub>b</sub> = p&#770; + &epsilon;<sub>n</sub>r<sub>n</sub>(p&#770;<sup>b</sup> &minus; p&#770;) is first screened:
   its distance to the RUM point fitted at p&#770; bounds dist(p<sub>b</sub>) from above, and a draw whose bound is below
   &tau;<sub>lo</sub> is decided *below* with no solve. Any other draw restarts from the p&#770; solution with two threshold
   stops (*below* once its certified UB &lt; &tau;<sub>lo</sub>, *above* once its certified LB &ge; &tau;<sub>hi</sub>); a draw that
   is neither after two tenfold tighter rounds counts as undecided (1). The p-value is [n_above, n_above + n_undecided] / B;
   reject at 5 % iff the upper end is at most 0.05.

5. **Solvers.**
   - **BM-ADMM** with &beta; = 5, an upper bound (the pushed iterate) every 25 iterations and
     a Frank&ndash;Wolfe lower bound at the iterate every 100, float64.
   - **CGBFQP-GPU** with the solver's settings (float32 pricing, at most 4,000 columns); its bracket is recomputed in
     float64.

6. **Runs.** Eight runs, one process each, on an idle RTX 5090, 2026-10-23 and 2026-10-25. Each record keeps every draw's bracket.

- **Every draw is decided below, so p = 0 and RUM is rejected on all four data sets.** No draw comes near the
  threshold: every draw's screen bound is at most 0.56, 0.68, 0.24 and 0.30 of &tau;<sub>lo</sub> (gpt-5.6-luna, deepseek-v4-flash, gpt-6-luna-noreason and gmi-cloud), so the screen decides all of them and no draw is solved.
- **The certificate.** BM-ADMM certifies in 11.4 s, 10.9 s, 13.0 s and 19.5 s (13,800, 13,200, 14,850 and 23,425 iterations); CGBFQP-GPU needs 25.2 s, 94.3 s, 15.2 s and 435.2 s (699, 1,416, 501 and 2,640 columns), in the order
  of the table.
- **The draws.** Building and screening all 10,000 draws takes 10.5 s to 10.6 s on the 100-reply data sets and 90.8 s to 111 s (1.9 min) on the
  2,000-reply ones, whose 2,000 pseudo-respondents give each draw 20 times as many replies to count.
- **The interval** needs certified lower bounds on the upper tail of the draws: on 100 replicates about 630
  solves to abs 5e-4 at about 7 s each with BM-ADMM, so about 1.3 h (measured on 80 gpt-5.6-luna draws). It is
  not computed here.

## The two-sided 95 % interval for dist

The interval of the paper's application section is [max(0, LB &minus; c), UB + c]: the certified bracket on
dist(p&#770;) widened by c, the 95 % quantile of &Vert;p&#770;<sup>b</sup> &minus; p&#770;&Vert; over the draws. It holds because
|dist(p) &minus; dist(p&#770;)| &le; &Vert;p &minus; p&#770;&Vert; for the distance to any convex set, so it needs no draw solved
(`Bootstrap.distance_interval()`). Its draws are menu by menu, as the data were made: each menu's replies are
resampled on their own (`run_bootstrap.py --input replies`, multinomial weights, B = 10,000, seed 0), not tied
together by replicate. One BM-ADMM run per data set, with the settings above; the p-value of these draws is 0 as well, every draw decided below by the screen:

| data | dist(p&#770;) in [LB, UB] | c | 95 % interval | p-value | total |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| gpt-5.6-luna | [13.510657, 13.511156] | 6.9326 | **[6.578, 20.444]** | 0 | 42.0 s |
| deepseek-v4-flash | [11.151396, 11.151895] | 7.6143 | **[3.537, 18.766]** | 0 | 52.6 s |
| gpt-6-luna-noreason | [13.389228, 13.389728] | 1.4472 | **[11.942, 14.837]** | 0 | 45.2 s |
| gmi-cloud | [7.220187, 7.220685] | 1.7673 | **[5.453, 8.988]** | 0 | 68.7 s |

- **The paper's intervals** are reproduced: gpt-6-luna-noreason [11.942, 14.837] and gmi-cloud [5.453, 8.988] (paper: [11.942, 14.836] and [5.453, 8.988]).
- **The 100-reply data sets have the widest intervals:** c shrinks roughly as 1/&radic;n, n the replies per menu.

## Criterion compared with DSR

Both applications run the same procedure: abs 5e-4, the screen, and up to two tenfold tighter rounds for the draws
still undecided. DSR uses the rounds, because some DSR draws sit close to their threshold; the AI runs never do,
because no AI draw does. Relative to the distance, 5e-4 on the AI data is about as tight as 1e-4 on DSR, since the
AI distances are 3 to 9 times larger:

| application | data | dist(p&#770;) | abs tol | abs / dist(p&#770;) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| AI | gpt-5.6-luna | 13.51 | 5e-4 | 3.7e-05 |
| AI | deepseek-v4-flash | 11.15 | 5e-4 | 4.5e-05 |
| AI | gpt-6-luna-noreason | 13.39 | 5e-4 | 3.7e-05 |
| AI | gmi-cloud | 7.22 | 5e-4 | 6.9e-05 |
| DSR | alt0 | 1.489 | 5e-4, refined to 5e-6 | 3.4e-04, refined 3.4e-06 |
| DSR | full | 2.299 | 5e-4, refined to 5e-6 | 2.2e-04, refined 2.2e-06 |

## CGIP and CGIP-heur

The CSV also has the 4 CPU cluster runs of CGIP and CGIP-heur on gpt-5.6-luna and deepseek-v4-flash (24 Gurobi
threads), made with an earlier version of the CGIP code (`replication/cgip/cluster_records/Application_AI/`;
`replication/cgip/run_cgip.py` re-runs them with this release). None finished the dist(p&#770;) certificate within
the 24 h wall limit, so none drew a bootstrap sample:

| data | solver | note |
|---|---|---|
| gpt-5.6-luna | CGIP | killed at the 24 h limit during the dist(p_hat) solve; 6 iterations, no lower bound, no bootstrap draws; best UB 21.948861 |
| gpt-5.6-luna | CGIP-heur | R = 10 restarts; killed at the 24 h limit during the dist(p_hat) solve; 86 heuristic iterations then stuck in the first exact pricing IP; no lower bound, no bootstrap draws; best UB 14.076758 |
| deepseek-v4-flash | CGIP | killed at the 24 h limit during the dist(p_hat) solve; 3 iterations, no lower bound, no bootstrap draws; best UB 35.521975 |
| deepseek-v4-flash | CGIP-heur | R = 10 restarts; killed at the 24 h limit during the dist(p_hat) solve; 142 heuristic iterations then stuck in the first exact pricing IP; no lower bound, no bootstrap draws; best UB 11.703079 |

## Reproduce

    python replication/empirical_ai_n18/run_bootstrap.py --model luna --no-ci
    python replication/empirical_ai_n18/run_bootstrap.py --model luna --method flow_cg --no-ci --tag _flow_cg
    python replication/empirical_ai_n18/run_bootstrap.py --model deepseek --no-ci              # and with flow_cg
    python replication/empirical_ai_n18/run_bootstrap.py --model gpt6luna_noreason --no-ci     # and with flow_cg
    python replication/empirical_ai_n18/run_bootstrap.py --model gmicloud --no-ci     # and with flow_cg
    python replication/empirical_ai_n18/run_bootstrap.py --model gmicloud --input replies --no-ci --distance-interval
        # the two-sided interval; the same for luna, deepseek and gpt6luna_noreason
    python validation/make_final_results_apps.py
    python validation/write_final_results_readmes.py

Without `--no-ci` a run also computes the 95 % interval.
